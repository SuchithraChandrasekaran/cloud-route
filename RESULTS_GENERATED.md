## Found while building this set

An earlier version of the batch-size check in `agent/evaluate.py` required the overhead heuristic to rise with every smaller cap. This set showed that is not always true: on 33 of these 550 graphs a cap of 3 scored higher than a cap of 2, because both give the same number of phases and the score depends on how the components fall into them. The engine was correct; the claim was wrong. The check now tests only the relations that held on every graph: the phase count never falls as the cap shrinks, every capped plan scores at least the uncapped plan, and cap 1 scores at least every other cap.

## Limits

- These graphs are synthetic. They test shape variety: deep chains, wide fans, dense and sparse graphs, hubs, disconnected components. They do not show that the engine handles a real system; the bank platform dataset and the live cluster are the realistic cases.
- 52 of the 550 graphs have no dependencies at all (the 'isolated' shape), so the injected-cycle check does not apply to them. Every other graph is tested with one injected cycle.
- Each graph gets one injected cycle, not 200. The 200-trial cycle test runs on the bank dataset and the live cluster (see RESULTS.md).
- The checks show the output is correct and safe, not that it is the cheapest possible order. The overhead heuristic is not validated against real costs.

Cases are fixed by (shape, seed), so a failure can be reproduced with `generate_case(shape, seed)` from `agent/evaluate_generated.py`.
