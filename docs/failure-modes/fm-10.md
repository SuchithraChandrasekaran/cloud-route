title: A shared dependency fails and its dependents fail with it
keywords: shared dependency, blast radius, dependents, cascade, failure, rollback scope, many services depend, single point of failure, outage

When a component that many others rely on has a problem, every component that depends on it, directly or through another component, is affected. The scope of a rollback is that component plus everything that depends on it. The more components depend on one, the larger that scope, so a failure in a widely used component costs more to undo than one in a component nothing depends on.
