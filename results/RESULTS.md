## What is being tested

## Limits of this evaluation

- Only 7 of the 70 dataset components are deployed. The other 63 are modeled to give the engine a graph of realistic size and depth. The results show the engine is correct on a graph of that shape, not that a real 70-service system was migrated.
- Dependencies on the live cluster are inferred from Service selectors and `cloud-route/depends-on` annotations. A dependency that appears in neither is invisible to the engine.
- "Cheapest first" orders by a cost proxy (requested CPU and memory), not a cloud bill. On the live cluster all deployments request the same resources, so their order within a phase is a tie.
- The overhead heuristic is not validated against real migration costs. It is not part of the pass/fail results above.
- The checks show the engine's output is correct and safe. They do not show it is the cheapest possible order.
- The Nemotron explanations are not evaluated here; they are covered by the grounding checks in `tests/`.
